### Essential Model Answer

> "The OSI model is a framework for understanding network communication. Physical handles signals and cables, Data Link handles local frames and MAC addresses, Network handles IP addressing and routing, Transport handles end-to-end delivery using TCP or UDP, and the upper layers support sessions, data formatting, and applications. In troubleshooting, I move from the physical layer upward."
