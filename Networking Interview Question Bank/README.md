### Recommended Answer Structure

> **Note:** The time shown for each question is the recommended maximum answer time. 

For most technical questions, structure your response as follows:

1. **Define** the concept.
2. **Provide** a practical example.
3. **Explain** how you would verify or troubleshoot it.
4. **Connect** it to your experience where relevant.
